# Our Story

Hi! We're two sisters who have always shared two big loves: candles and animals.

One day, while scrolling through reels of animal shelters, we found ourselves asking the same question: *How can we help in a way that's sustainable?* We wanted to build something that people would genuinely love while also making a meaningful, long-term impact. That's when the idea for our candle business was born.

It all started with one test candle. From there came countless late nights experimenting with wax blends, testing fragrances, refining designs, and learning through plenty of trial and error. What began as a small passion project slowly grew into a brand that lights homes, celebrates special moments, and brings people together—and none of it would have been possible without your support.

Every candle we create is still lovingly handcrafted. Each decorative candle is individually hand-poured, while our jar candles are made using **100% pure soy wax** for a clean, beautiful burn. Our decorative candles are crafted from a carefully developed **soy and paraffin wax blend**, allowing them to hold their intricate details and withstand Pakistan's warm climate without compromising on quality.

The most meaningful part of this journey, however, is what it allows us to give back.

A portion of every order goes towards supporting animal shelters, helping provide food, medical care, and shelter for animals waiting for a second chance. We are proud to support **ACF** and **ARTS**, two organizations doing incredible work to care for and rescue animals in need.

When you choose our candles, you're doing more than supporting a small business. You're helping us continue a mission that has been close to our hearts from the very beginning.

Thank you for believing in our dream, for choosing handmade, and for helping us create something that's about so much more than candles. Together, we're making homes a little warmer and the world a little kinder—one candle at a time.
